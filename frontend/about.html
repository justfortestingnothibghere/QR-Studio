About QR Studio

QR Studio turns a plain QR code into a poster you are happy to put on a shop counter, a cafe table or a story. It is built and run by TeamDev, a small team of developers in India.

Why you can trust it

  • You see a free preview first. Nothing is charged until you decide.
  • Payment is by UPI only. We never ask for your card, bank login or UPI PIN.
  • Your payment is matched automatically from the bank alert, so there is nothing to upload.
  • Every preview is scan-checked. The final file is checked again before you download.
  • Passwords are hashed. Payment data is kept only to match your order.
