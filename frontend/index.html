Questions people ask

Is the preview really free?

Yes. You can build, restyle and preview as much as you like. Only the watermark-free download costs money.

How do you confirm my payment?

You pay the exact amount shown for your order. Payment apps send a confirmation email that contains a 12-digit UTR. You paste that UTR and we match it with the payment, the amount and the time.

Will my QR still scan?

We run a scan check on every design. If a style or logo makes the code hard to read you see a warning, so you can switch to plain black ink or remove the logo.

Can I use my own artwork?

Yes. Upload a portrait image in the Theme Maker, choose a layout and it shows up next to the built-in themes. Only use artwork you have the right to use.
